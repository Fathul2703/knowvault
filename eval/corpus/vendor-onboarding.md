# Vendor Onboarding

New vendors are onboarded by the procurement team before their first purchase order.

## Required documents

Each vendor submits a signed supplier agreement, a tax registration certificate and the details
of a business bank account. Procurement checks the documents within five working days.

## Payment terms

Invoices from approved vendors are paid thirty days after the invoice date. Vendors can ask for
early payment, which is granted only with a two percent discount.
