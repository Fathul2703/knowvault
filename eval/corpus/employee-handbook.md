# Employee Handbook

This handbook collects the everyday rules of working at the company. It complements the separate
policies on leave, travel, passwords and data retention, which take precedence where they cover
the same topic. The people team updates the handbook once a year and announces every change in the
company channel.

## Who this handbook is for

The handbook applies to everyone employed by the company, in every office and in every country,
including people who work fully remotely. Contractors follow the parts on security, office access
and conduct; their contracts cover everything else. Where local law gives employees more than the
handbook does, local law applies.

## Working hours

The standard working week is forty hours. You can organise your day as it suits you and your team,
but the core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone: during core hours you are expected
to be reachable, and recurring meetings are only scheduled within them. Outside core hours,
nobody is expected to answer messages, and managers should not send work requests late in the
evening or at weekends.

If you need to be away during core hours for an appointment, block the time in your calendar and
tell your team. There is no need to ask for permission for short absences.

## Remote work

Most roles can be done partly from home. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week,
agreed with their manager, and come to the office on the other days. Teams agree on at least one
shared office day per week so that people actually meet.

Working from another country for more than four weeks in a year needs approval from the people
team, because of tax and insurance rules. Short stays abroad do not need approval, but your
manager should know where you are working from.

### Home office setup

When you start, you can claim a one-time home office allowance of 300 euros for a desk, a chair or
lighting. Keep the receipts and submit them as an expense claim within three months of your start
date. The allowance is not paid out in cash and cannot be claimed again after moving house.

## Office access

Every employee receives a personal access badge on the first day. The badge opens the doors of the
offices you work in and is also used to print. Never lend your badge to anyone, not even to a
colleague who forgot theirs; reception can issue a day pass instead.

Report a lost badge to reception at once so that it can be blocked. A replacement badge costs
fifteen euros, which are deducted from the next salary payment. A badge damaged through normal use
is replaced free of charge.

## Visitors

Register visitors at least one working day in advance in the visitor system, with their full name
and the name of their company. Visitors receive a temporary badge at reception and must be
accompanied by their host at all times outside the meeting rooms on the ground floor. Visitors may
not enter the server room or the archive under any circumstances.

Deliveries are not visitors: couriers leave packages at reception and do not need to be registered.

## Equipment

The company provides the equipment you need for your role, such as a computer, a monitor, a
headset and a keyboard. Request additional equipment, such as a second monitor or an ergonomic
chair, through the facilities request form; requests are normally fulfilled within ten working
days.

Equipment remains the property of the company. When you leave, return all equipment within five
working days after your last day; the people team sends prepaid boxes to people who work remotely.

## Information security in daily work

Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk; computers also lock automatically after five
minutes without activity. Do not connect USB storage devices to company computers; use the shared
drives instead. Suspicious emails are reported with the Report phishing button in the mail client,
which forwards them to the security team. Never forward a suspicious email to colleagues to ask
whether it is genuine.

Confidential documents may only be printed on the secure printers, which release a print job when
you present your badge.

## Learning and development

Every employee has a personal learning budget of 1,000 euros per year for courses, books and
conference tickets related to their work. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.
Spending above 300 euros for a single item needs your manager's agreement first.

In addition, everyone may spend up to five working days a year on learning during working time, for
example to attend a conference or to complete a course.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in May and November. They consist of a
self-assessment, feedback from two colleagues of your choice, and a conversation with your manager.
Salary adjustments are discussed only in the November review.

New employees have their first review at the end of their probation period instead of at the next
regular date.

## Probation period

The probation period is three months for all roles. During probation, either side can end the
employment with a notice period of one week. At the end of probation, your manager confirms your
employment in writing after the review.

## Internal mobility

You can apply for another role in the company after twelve months in your current role, or earlier
if your manager agrees. Internal candidates are interviewed for every role they apply for and are
told the outcome before external candidates are contacted.

## Conduct

Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind
are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with your manager, with the people team, or anonymously
through the integrity line, which is run by an external provider. Raising a concern in good faith
never leads to disadvantages for the person who raised it.

Gifts from customers or suppliers worth more than 50 euros must be declined or reported to the
compliance team.

## Leaving the company

The notice period for resignations is one week during probation and two months after probation,
always ending at the end of a calendar month. Please hand over your work in writing and agree an
exit date with your manager. On your last day, return your badge at reception; your accounts are
closed at the end of that day.

## Questions

For anything this handbook does not answer, contact the people team through the help portal.
Requests are answered within two working days.
