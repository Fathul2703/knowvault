# Sourdough Baking Notes

These are notes from a year of baking sourdough bread at home.

## Starter

Feed the starter with equal weights of flour and water. A starter is ready to use when it has
doubled in size within four to six hours after feeding and smells pleasantly sour. Keep it in
the refrigerator between bakes and feed it once a week.

## Dough

The basic loaf uses 500 grams of bread flour, 350 grams of water, 100 grams of active starter
and 10 grams of salt. Mix flour and water first and let them rest for one hour before adding
the starter; this rest, called autolyse, makes the dough easier to stretch.

## Bulk fermentation

Do four sets of stretch and folds, thirty minutes apart. Bulk fermentation is finished when the
dough has grown by about half and shows bubbles on the sides of the bowl. In a warm kitchen this
takes about four hours, in a cold one up to eight.

## Baking

Bake in a preheated cast-iron pot at 250 degrees Celsius for twenty minutes with the lid on,
then another twenty minutes without the lid. Let the bread cool for at least one hour before
cutting it, or the crumb will be gummy.
