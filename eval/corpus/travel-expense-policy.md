# Travel and Expense Policy

Employees may travel for customer visits, conferences and team gatherings. This policy explains
what is reimbursed and how to claim it.

## Booking

Book flights and hotels through the travel portal at least fourteen days in advance when
possible. Economy class is the default; business class is allowed only for flights longer than
eight hours. Hotels should cost no more than the city rate published in the portal.

## Meals and daily allowance

A daily meal allowance covers breakfast, lunch and dinner on travel days. If a meal is included
in a conference ticket, that meal is deducted from the allowance. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Claims

Submit expense claims within 30 days after the trip with a photo of every receipt. Claims
without receipts are reimbursed only up to a small amount and need approval from the manager.
Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Unused time off during travel

Travel on a weekend does not create extra leave days. If a trip makes you work on a public
holiday, agree a replacement day with your manager before the trip.
